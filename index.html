ABOUT THE COMPANY

Connecting suppliers and buyers across international markets.

AGROTRADE MILENIUM S.L. is a Barcelona-based international trading and supply company engaged in the wholesale and export of agro-products and recyclable raw materials.

Our activities include sourcing, consolidation, supply and international movement of fruits and vegetables, ferrous and non-ferrous metals, plastic scrap, paper scrap and related recyclable materials.

We work with commercial and industrial buyers according to product specifications, quantity, quality, origin, destination and applicable regulations.

Non-Ferrous Scrap

High-value recyclable metals supplied according to alloy and buyer specifications.

  • Aluminum — UBC, tense/taint, sheet & extrusion
  • Aluminum wheels, cast aluminum & turnings
  • Copper — bare bright, #1, #2 & tubing
  • Insulated copper wire & cable
  • Brass & bronze scrap
  • Copper/brass & aluminum radiators
  • Stainless steel — 304, 316 & other grades
  • Zinc, lead, nickel, magnesium & titanium
